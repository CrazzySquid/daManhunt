Rules

Setup

Usually 2 runners vs. 3–7 hunters. Runners must stay together (ca. 50m) at all times — if one is caught, both are out. Hunters work as one team.

Boundaries

Nobody may leave Vienna. No entering private buildings, hospitals, schools, construction sites etc.

Transport

Walking, running and public transport are allowed. No cars or other private means of transport (also no E-Scooters).

Head Start

Runners get 15–20 minutes with zero tracking before hunters may move. The Runners objectives are not revealed, until the end of that period.

Win as Runner

After head start, a few (3-7) objectives are generated out of an openly available list of roughly 30 locations. In order to win, runners need to visit all of them before the time runs out.

Win as Hunter

In order to win, the runners must be caught by a hunter, or time runs out before the runners manage to get to all of their locations.

Finale

Once every objective except the last has been visited, that final location is immediately revealed to everyone — hunters must catch the runners there before they reach it.

Maps

Runners may check their own current location on a map, but never use Google Maps etc. to search up and navigate to a location.

Capture

Hunters win by taking a clear photo of one of the runners, or by making direct contact with one of the runners.
